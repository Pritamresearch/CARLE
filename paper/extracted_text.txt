1 Introduction
Autonomous vehicles (AVs) navigating dense and unstructured trafficcharacter-
istic of Asia-Pacific environmentsrely on real-time semantic scene understand-
ing for safe manoeuvring. Collaborative driving, where vehicles share percep-
tual representations to improve situational awareness, has emerged as a key
paradigm for handling scenarios beyond single-sensor reach [1,2]. The standard
approach transmits full-resolution semantic segmentation masks over Vehicle-
to-Everything (V2X) links. Under 3GPP TR 38.885 in urban microcell con-
straints, this is prohibitively bandwidth-intensive [3]: a single900×1600mask
at 8 bits/pixel requires 11,520 Kb per frame, and modern AVs carry 6 or more
cameras, yielding a combined raw bitrate exceeding 69,120 Kb/frame.
Existing approaches address this in two ways, both with significant limita-
tions in all of them. Communication-efficient collaborative perception schemes
such as Where2comm [4] and feature compression methods based on heavy back-
bones such as ResNet-101 or PointNet++ [1] reduce raw data volume but re-
quire full mask reconstruction or deep decoding at the receiver, imposing latency

2 Methodology
2.1 Scene Simplification
Let the input video be a sequence of frames{It}T t=1, whereI t ∈R H×W×3. Each
frame is processed by a pre-trained segmentation modelSto produce a full
label mapF t ∈Z H×W. To remove semantic noise and focus transmission on
safety-critical content, all classes are projected onto:
Ccritical ={c 1 =road, c2 =vehicle, c3 =pedestrian, c4 =other}(1)
yielding a simplified mask ˜Ft where non-critical pixels map toc 4. This four-
class representation retains all collision-relevant semantics while reducing label
entropy.

frame and updated by writing each triplet(s k, lk, ck)into the corresponding

3 Experiments
3.1 Datasets and Evaluation Setup
Experiments are conducted under CPU-only edge constraints with no GPU ac-
celeration. The nuScenes Mini dataset [15] consists of 2,424 frame pairs across
10 scenes and 6 cameras at900×1600resolution, using YOLO11n-seg as the
upstream segmentation model (Braw = 11,520Kb/frame). The field-test dataset
includes 1,073 frames from 4 real-world driving videos at848×478resolution
(3,166.8 Kb/frame), covering static, mixed, and dense traffic conditions. The
Cityscapes Panoptic Parts dataset [16], built on the Cityscapes benchmark [17],
contains1,487consecutiveground-truthmaskpairsacross18citiesat1024×2048
resolution (16,384≈Kb/frame). The implementation is publicly available at
https://github.com/Pritamresearch/carle-v2x.

5 Conclusion
This paper presented CARLE (Class-Aware Run-Length Encoding), a determin-
istic, training-free framework that compresses semantic segmentation masks for
V2X collaborative perception by transmitting only inter-frame label changes as
startlengthclass triplets, with unchanged pixels set to a reserved null symbol (0),
enabling direct receiver-side map updates without decoding. Evaluated across
three CPU-only datasets2,424 nuScenes Mini frames, 1,073 field-test frames,
and 1,487 Cityscapes panoptic pairsCARLE achieves 265.1×at 60.40 ms, 884×
